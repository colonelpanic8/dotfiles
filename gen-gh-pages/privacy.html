Privacy Policy

This Google Cloud OAuth application is a personal tool operated by Ivan Malison for his own accounts. It is not offered to the public.

It reads Gmail, Google Workspace, and Google Health data only for the signed-in account, on that account owner's own machines, to automate personal tasks such as email handling and habit tracking. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Credentials are stored locally in an encrypted password store. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
